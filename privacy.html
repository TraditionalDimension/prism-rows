This Privacy Policy applies to Prism Rows, published by TraditionalDimension under the package name com.traddim.prismrows.

Data collection and sharing

Prism Rows does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The game contains no advertising, analytics SDK, user account system, social features, or in-app purchases.

Data stored on the device

Game progress, achievements, run state, and settings are stored locally on the device so the game can save progress and preferences. This information is not sent to TraditionalDimension. It can be removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

External links

The game opens the Privacy Policy and Support page in the device's external browser. Desktop editions may also show a link to TraditionalDimension's Boosty page. Prism Rows does not receive browsing data from those services. Their own terms and privacy policies apply after you leave the game.

Children

Prism Rows is intended for players aged 13 and older. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This page may be updated if the game or its data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when appropriate.
